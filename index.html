About Our Company

Evelynx Solutions
Private Limited

Headquartered in Nepal, Evelynx Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a technology-driven enterprise with a primary focus on delivering world-class IT services. Our main objects encompass Software Development, Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, Financial Technology (Fintech), Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), and Professional IT Training.

In addition to our core services, we pursue ancillary objects including IT consulting, hardware procurement & supply, digital marketing solutions, and maintenance of computer systems — enabling us to serve as a comprehensive technology partner for businesses across Nepal and beyond.

⚖️ Structured as a Limited Liability Company — members' liability is limited, ensuring secure and transparent corporate governance.
