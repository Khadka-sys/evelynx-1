About Evelynx Solutions

Headquartered in Kathmandu, Nepal, Evelynx Solutions Private Limited is a technology company built on the foundation of limited liability and local expertise. We offer a comprehensive range of IT services — from software development, cloud, and cybersecurity to wholesale & retail of computer hardware, IT training, and equipment repair.

Our team understands the Nepali market and delivers solutions that meet both international standards and local business needs.
