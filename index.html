Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Managed IT Service? +

We take full responsibility for your IT infrastructure, from monitoring and maintenance to security and support – all for a fixed monthly fee, allowing you to focus on your business.

How quickly can you respond to a support request? +

Our helpdesk is available 24/7. Most issues are acknowledged within 15 minutes, and critical problems are resolved in under 4 hours, thanks to our remote and on‑site teams.

Yes! While our office is in Kathmandu, we serve clients across Nepal – from Pokhara to Biratnagar – with a combination of remote support and on‑site visits.

Absolutely. We use bank‑grade encryption, regular backups, and follow strict compliance standards. Your data never leaves Nepal without your explicit consent.
