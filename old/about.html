About Evelynx Solutions

Rooted in Nepal,
built for the world.

Evelynx Solutions Private Limited is a Kathmandu‑based technology company offering a complete spectrum of IT services. From software publishing and cloud architecture to wholesale hardware and equipment repair, we provide end‑to‑end solutions under one roof.

Our company is structured as a limited liability entity, ensuring transparency, security, and trust for all stakeholders. We combine deep local expertise with international best practices to deliver value that matters.
